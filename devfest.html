DevFest Carnival 2025 brought together student communities for workshops, showcases, creative stalls, food, games, music, and performances. Organized by Herald DevCorps, the carnival celebrated months of student-led events and collaboration.

As Itinerary Lead, I helped plan and coordinate the event schedule and activities. I also earned Top Scorer recognition at DevFest 2025. Both experiences gave me a chance to contribute behind the scenes while taking part in a vibrant campus celebration.
